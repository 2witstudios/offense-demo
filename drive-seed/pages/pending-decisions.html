Pending decisions

Decisions an agent made on the owner's behalf. Each is a DEC-n task created by bun decision:record "<decision>" --context <pageId> [--why "<reason>"], linked to where it was made, and announced in {{page:epicUpdates}}.

Rules

  • A decision stays open (To Do) until the owner moves it to Confirmed or Overruled. Agents never close a decision.
  • Work may proceed on the decision while it is open; an Overruled decision becomes a follow-up leaf or issue that undoes it.
  • Never bury a decision as "please confirm" in prose, a handoff or a PR comment — record it here.
