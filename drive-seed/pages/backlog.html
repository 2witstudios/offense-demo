Backlog

Feature candidates awaiting a spec. Grooming promotes a candidate into an epic leaf — create the leaf in its epic referencing this candidate's ID, then mark the candidate Done — nothing moves folders. A candidate becomes Ready only as an epic leaf, when it is committed for work.

Rules

  • Candidates are problems or outcomes, one line each; acceptance criteria get written when the leaf is specced.
  • Priority is the grooming order signal; the Scrum Master grooms top-down during planning.
